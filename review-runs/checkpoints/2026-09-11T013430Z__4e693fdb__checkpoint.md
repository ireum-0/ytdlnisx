Therefore two valid Sina source URLs with identical scheme/host/path/query but different extractor-recognized fragment IDs map to the same destructive History duplicate key. If their `DownloadType` is equal, `getDuplicateGroups()` groups them and `deleteDuplicates()` retains one and deletes the other History row/playlist references.
