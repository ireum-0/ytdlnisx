Affected production path: `app/src/main/java/com/ireum/ytdl/database/viewmodel/SettingsViewModel.kt`, with supporting contracts in `BackupSettingsUtil`, `DownloadCacheOwnership`, and `CleanupScheduleCoordinator`.

Disposition: existing `BUG-CLEANUP-01/F10`, P2 OPEN.
