- evidence A, terminal successor loss: `finishRunAndSchedule()` first persists `runInProgress=false` and empty `currentRunStatus`, then calls `enqueueUniqueWork("OBSERVE$sourceID", REPLACE, request)` and discards the returned WorkManager `Operation`, immediately returning `Result.success()`. - evidence B, cancel->enqueue race: `ObserveSourcesRepository.observeTask()` calls `cancelObservationTaskByID()`, which issues `cancelUniqueWork` and two `cancelAllWorkByTag` requests without awaiting their Operations, then immediately enqueues a replacement carrying the same unique name/tags. AndroidX documents cancellation as best-effort and exposes Operation completion. A delayed tag/name cancellation can overlap the replacement handoff; no completion barrier proves the old cancellation is finished before the new carrier is established.
- safe controls proving intended contract: `ObserveRetryDecisionReceiver` explicitly captures `enqueueUniqueWork` Operation and awaits `operation.result`; cleanup scheduling separately requires `CleanupScheduleCoordinator.scheduleSuccessor()` acceptance before treating handoff as accepted or retains exact recovery ownership when it is not accepted.
- disposition: OPEN, remediation required. The scheduler handoff must either await/consume Operation completion before terminal success and before cancel->replacement sequencing, or persist an exact durable recovery responsibility that survives process death and can rediscover/re-enqueue the exact source generation.
