## Existing open P2
The current Terminal worker correctly detects stranded current-attempt output after a partial move and its failure cleanup no longer recursively deletes that remainder. Instead, `cleanupTerminalOutputDirectory()` calls `TerminalCacheOwnership.revokeOwnershipPreservingArtifacts()`, removing the ownership marker while retaining the artifact manifest and files.

That preserved state is not production-discoverable for recovery/import. `TerminalCacheOwnership.listOwnedRoots()` accepts only directories with a valid owner marker. `CacheImportPlanner.collect()` constructs Terminal candidates exclusively from `TerminalCacheOwnership.listOwnedRoots()`. Consequently, after the worker deliberately revokes the marker, the exact preserved remainder is filtered out before `listArtifactFiles()` can consume its manifest.

No separate markerless Terminal reconciler was established in the reviewed production storage/recovery path. Physical survival therefore remains distinct from recoverability. This is the existing BUG-TERMINAL-04 / BUG-OUTPUT partial-publication recovery obligation, not a new canonical finding.
