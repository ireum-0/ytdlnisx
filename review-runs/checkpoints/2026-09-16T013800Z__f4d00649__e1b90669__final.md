### Concrete production evidence
`CleanUpLeftoverDownloads : CoroutineWorker` calls `setForegroundAsync(ForegroundInfo(...))` and discards the returned `ListenableFuture<Void>`, then immediately proceeds to destructive-effect admission and cleanup. The call is outside the worker's cleanup try/catch, and no future listener/await/join/durable responsibility observes its completion or failure.

The app pins `androidx.work:work-runtime-ktx:2.11.0`. AndroidX's `ListenableWorker.setForegroundAsync` contract states that calls must complete before the worker signals completion and that the returned future resolves only after transition to foreground. Current AndroidX `WorkForegroundUpdater` performs the transition asynchronously, throws if the WorkSpec is absent/finished, and propagates foreground-service restriction failures to that future. Official Kotlin `CoroutineWorker` guidance uses the suspending `setForeground()` API.

Therefore a foreground transition can still be pending or can fail while `CleanUpLeftoverDownloads` mutates Room/cache state. A later successful cleanup/successor path can return a WorkManager `Result` without ever consuming the foreground-transition failure. This violates the exact async completion contract and leaves no typed/durable failure owner for that platform prerequisite.

### Cross-feature propagation
`MoveCacheFilesWorker : CoroutineWorker` has the same ignored `setForegroundAsync(...)` pattern and then enters `CacheMaintenanceAuthority.withMaintenanceWindow` to move exact cache artifacts to external storage. Thus the semantic bug pattern is not cleanup-specific; at least one additional production destructive/mutating worker reproduces it.

Affected files confirmed at the frozen SHA:
- `app/src/main/java/com/ireum/ytdl/work/CleanUpLeftoverDownloads.kt`
- `app/src/main/java/com/ireum/ytdl/work/MoveCacheFilesWorker.kt`
- `app/build.gradle` (WorkManager 2.11.0 dependency / targetSdk 36 context)

Recommended semantic correction: in `CoroutineWorker`, use suspending `setForeground(ForegroundInfo(...))` before the first dependent mutation, or equivalently await/consume the future with explicit cancellation/failure semantics. Then propagate foreground-transition failure into the worker's typed retry/failure policy before destructive work begins. Audit every production `setForegroundAsync` callsite for the same contract.
