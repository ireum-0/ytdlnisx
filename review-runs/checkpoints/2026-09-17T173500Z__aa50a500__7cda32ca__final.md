### WORKER-FOREGROUND-COMPLETION-01 — P2 OPEN
Violated invariants: v6 core 9 (async request is not completion), 11/18 consumer/effect closure, Master Plan execution/platform barrier requirements.
Production paths/files: `CleanUpLeftoverDownloads.kt`, `UpdateMultipleDownloadsFormatsWorker.kt`, `MoveCacheFilesWorker.kt`.
Evidence: each is a Kotlin `CoroutineWorker`; each issues `setForegroundAsync(ForegroundInfo(...))` and does not await/chain the returned future before its first correctness-relevant effect. Cleanup proceeds to generation-owned destructive admission/journal execution; bulk-format proceeds to result/download durable mutation; MoveCache proceeds to maintenance-window filesystem moves. Disposition OPEN; existing finding, no new root/subcase this run.
