History stability and filtering improvements.

- Added audio/video type chips to the History filters.
- Reduced manual RecyclerView refreshes in the paged History list to prevent item-position inconsistency crashes.
- Improved History empty state, scroll restore, and local-video add progress updates.
- Improved playback fallback for SAF-backed local files and audio history items.
- Hardened yt-dlp option handling, shared intents, exported components, FileProvider scope, and SAF deletion behavior.
- Improved WorkManager foreground handling, download lifecycle ownership, cache moves, alarm cancellation, duplicate detection, and failure privacy.
